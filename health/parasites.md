# Parasites

## Enzymes

2 times a day 2 hours after or 1 hour before food. Dosage per day:

- Proteolytic enzymes: 6000-8000 FIP or 60000-80000 HUT
- Serrapeptase: 120000-200000 SPU

## Probiotics and Enterosorbents

2 times a day: morning and evening before food. Algae based enterosorbents could
be taken with probiotics, other types an hour before probiotics.

## Step 0: preparation - 7-20 days

- [NOW Foods, Digest Ultimate] - 2 capsules x 2-3 times a day
- [NOW Foods, Serrapeptase, 27mg] - 1 capsules x 2-3 times a day
- Probiotic - morning and evening without food
- Enterosorbents - Zosteraceae - with probiotic
- [Nutricost, Nattokinase, 100 mg (2,000 FU)] - 1 capsule before food

- [TUDCA - 800 mg - 1-2 times a day with food
- [NOW Foods, NAC With Selenium] - 600 mg - 1-2 with food
- Milk thistle oil - 1 capsule with food

## Step 1: testing - 3 days

- Albendazole - 400mg once a day
- Pyrantel - 10mg/kg of body weight, only first day with Albendazole
- Lactulose or inulin - 3mg x 2 times a day after food
- Enzymes 2 times a day with food
- Enterosorbents - Zosteraceae - with probiotic
- Coffee enema - 1-2 times a day in 3 hours after albendazole if intoxicated
